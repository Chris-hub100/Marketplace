How Selling Works

Your quick guide to making money safely on ProlyfiQ.

1. Stock Your Store

Add your items using the List New Item page. Make sure you set a fair price, but remember: the system automatically takes a 5% service fee when you get paid, so price accordingly!

Pro Tip: Use the Marketing Toolkit to easily share your storefront page on WhatsApp statuses and groups so students know what you are selling.

2. Get an Order

When a buyer wants your item, they pay online. We lock their money safely in the vault and instantly send you a notification with their phone number.

Next Move: Pack the item, call the buyer, and agree on a time to meet at your chosen campus landmark.

3. Meet & Scan

Meet the buyer and let them inspect the item. If they are happy with it, open your ProlyfiQ Dashboard and show them your QR Code for that item which can be found in the P.O.D./Withdrawals page .

CRITICAL: The buyer MUST scan your QR code with their phone. If they take the item without scanning, the system will not release your money!

4. Get Paid Instantly

The moment the buyer scans your code and swipes to confirms the delivery, the vault unlocks. Ensure you have a confirmation message on your device before leaving.

We deduct the 5% fee and instantly send the rest of the cash straight to your Mobile Money wallet at a flat GHC 0.40 fee. No waiting, no stress.
