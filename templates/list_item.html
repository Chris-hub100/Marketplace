How Variations Work

A quick guide to listing items with multiple options

1

Choose your variation type

Pick Storage / Size, Colour, Model, or define a custom label that best describes what differs between versions.

2

Add each variation one at a time

Enter a label (e.g. 256GB or Midnight Blue), its specific price in GHS, an optional description, and optional photos unique to that version.

3

Review the variation cards

Each added variation appears as a card below the entry form. You can remove any card and re-add it if needed.

4

Fill in the rest of the listing normally

The item name, stock, description, and logistics below apply to your whole listing. Per-variation prices take over the single price field automatically.

Finding it a bit tricky?

No problem at all. You can always toggle Variations off and list each version as its own independent item on the marketplace. Many sellers prefer that approach. Both work great.
