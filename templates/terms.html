Legal Framework for ProlyfiQ Services

1. Scope of Services

These Terms of Service apply to all platforms and tools operated by Ledgehold Enterprise (trading as ProlyfiQ), which include:

  • Campus Marketplace: Secure student-to-student buying and selling.
  • Utility Hub: Digital payments for Airtime, Data, ECG, DStv, and GOtv.
  • Logistics (FoodRun™): Campus delivery and errand services.
  • Merchant Tools: Sales tracking, inventory, and receipt generation.
  • TV Hub: Curated entertainment and discovery content.
The Gatekeeper Policy: No Proof, No Pay

To keep our marketplace safe and compliant, merchants are never paid automatically. You must provide proof that the item was delivered (such as the buyer successfully scanning your QR code) to receive your funds. Requests made without this proof will be denied.

2. Your Account & Security

We take your privacy seriously and have built the system so that you are in total control of your access.

  • Your PIN is Private: You create your own PIN. Our system scrambles it so deeply that no one at Ledgehold can see, retrieve, or guess it.
  • Your Responsibility: Because only you know your PIN, you are fully responsible for anything that happens in your merchant dashboard. If you think someone knows your PIN, you must reset it immediately.
  • Exact Match Required: Your login name must perfectly match the exact spelling and capitalization you used when you registered.
3. Bills & Top-Ups

All utility and airtime/data purchases are processed through our licensed partner, Hubtel.

  • No Reversals: Once a digital top-up or bill payment goes through, it is final and cannot be refunded.
  • Double-Check Your Numbers: If you accidentally type the wrong phone number or meter number, the funds cannot be recovered. ProlyfiQ is not responsible for these mistakes.
4. Delivery & FoodRun

Our logistics service connects you with student delivery agents to run errands.

  • Safe Meeting Spots: To keep everyone safe, handovers only happen at approved campus landmarks or official hostels. Delivery agents will only wait for a maximum of 10 minutes.
  • Food is Final: Because food is perishable, you cannot cancel or get a refund for a food order once the delivery agent has picked it up.
5. Buyer Protection & Refunds

When you buy something, ProlyfiQ holds the money safely until the meetup happens.

  • The Service Fee: We charge a 5% fee on completed sales to maintain our security systems.
  • Inspect Before You Accept: As a buyer, you must physically check the item when you meet the seller. Scanning the seller's QR code (or giving them your Delivery PIN) is your legal signature saying you are happy with the item. Once you do this, no refunds will be given.
  • Rejecting an Item: If you meet the seller and the item is broken or not what was promised, do not scan the code. We will refund the cost of the item (minus the non-refundable security fee) shortly after you report the issue.
  • Going Off-Platform: If you ignore the "Buy Now" button and decide to pay the seller directly with physical cash or your own MoMo, ProlyfiQ cannot protect you. We are not responsible for off-platform scams.
6. Buyer Delivery PINs & Security

To ensure high-level security during item collections, buyers are required to create a unique 4-digit Delivery PIN at checkout.

  • Cryptographic Protection: Just like your merchant account, your Delivery PIN is immediately encrypted into a secure digital signature before hitting our database. Nobody at Ledgehold can view your PIN.
  • The "MoMo" Rule: Treat your Delivery PIN like your Mobile Money PIN. Never share it with a merchant over the phone, text, or WhatsApp. You must only type it into the verification portal yourself when you have physically inspected and received the item.
  • Shared Device Safety: If you are collecting an item using a borrowed phone or a friend's browser, use the manual confirmation portal to safely isolate your account details and prevent your personal PIN from being saved to an external cache.
7. Content & External Links

The ProlyfiQ TV Hub helps you discover great movies and products.

  • Third-Party Videos: We stream public videos from platforms like YouTube. We do not own this content.
  • Amazon Affiliate Link: We are part of the Amazon Associates Program. If you click an Amazon link on our platform and buy something, we may earn a small commission at no extra cost to you.
8. Legal Limits of Our Responsibility

Ledgehold Enterprise provides the software platform to make campus trading safe and efficient. However, we do not manufacture the items sold by students, nor do we run the national telecommunication networks. Therefore, we are not legally or financially responsible if a purchased item breaks later, or if a network outage delays a service.

9. Governing Law

These terms follow the laws of the Republic of Ghana. If a serious legal dispute arises that cannot be solved through our support team, it will be settled through formal arbitration in Accra.
