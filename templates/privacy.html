Privacy Policy

Ledgehold's ProlyfiQ Enterprise Standards

Commitment to Act 843

ProlyfiQ operates in strict accordance with the Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843) of Ghana. We treat Student ID data and Merchant Access Keys as "Special Personal Data," ensuring maximum encryption and limited access to maintain the Ledgehold Standard.

What we see
  • Full Name & University Details
  • Uploaded Photos
  • Course of Study
  • MoMo Payout Numbers
  • Daily Logs & Verification Receipts
How we protect it
  • AES-256 Cloud Encryption
  • 6-hour Auto-Purge for ID uploads
  • 12-hour Auto-Purge for Photo uploads
Zero-Knowledge PIN Standards

To guarantee total data protection for our Users, Ledgehold utilizes a zero-knowledge architecture for dashboard access:

  • Non-Retrievable Keys: We do not store plain-text PINs. Your PIN is mathematically transformed into a hash that cannot be reversed.
  • Staff Access: Ledgehold employees, administrators, and developers have zero visibility into your private keys.
  • Security Mandate: In the event of a lost PIN, the existing record must be administratively revoked (wiped) and the merchant must re-onboard to generate a new key.
  • Session Security: To prevent unauthorized access on shared devices, active dashboard sessions automatically terminate after 2 minutes of inactivity.
The Gatekeeper Policy

ProlyfiQ (via Ledgehold) maintains a strict transparency mandate. Payout requests submitted without a corresponding proof of delivery will be flagged as ineligible for payout. This verification data is retained solely for compliance auditing and annual tax filing requirements.

Third-Party Disclosure

We use existing, enterprise tools for maximum reliability. Your data is securely routed through Cloudinary (Identity Verification) and Paystack (Financial Settlements). These providers are industry-standard and maintain their own rigorous security protocols.
